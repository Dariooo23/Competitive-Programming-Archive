# Competitive Programming Archive

A personal collection of algorithmic solutions, contest problems, and training materials gathered over years of active participation in programming competitions and academic courses.  

---

## 📁 Repository Structure

### 🧩 **Algorithms**
Implementations of fundamental and advanced algorithms used across contests and research.  
Includes data structures, graph theory, dynamic programming, math, geometry, and string algorithms.  
**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate → 🔴 Advanced  
**Purpose:** Reusable templates for competitive programming.

---

### 🏁 **Competitions**
Solutions and notes from various programming contests: **Olympiad in Informatics (OI)**, **CodeQuest**, **CodeForces**, **MistrzProgramowania**, **Solve4**
**Level:** 🔴 Advanced  
**Purpose:** Real contest problems with efficient, contest-tested solutions.

---

### 🧠 **Science Camps**
Materials and problems solved during algorithmic training camps and workshops.  
**Level:** 🟡 Intermediate → 🔴 Advanced  
**Purpose:** Deep dive into creative problem solving and non-standard algorithms.

---

### 🎓 **Studies**
Academic algorithms and structures developed during Computer Science at **Jagiellonian University**.   
**Level:** 🟢 Beginner → 🟡 Intermediate  
**Purpose:** Reinforcing academic understanding through implementation.
